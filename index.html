Who we are

An apostolic ministry building lives, and through them, nations

Harvest Time Ministries exists to raise believers who are grounded in the Word, mature in character, and sent out to shape the communities and nations they belong to. We believe the harvest is ready, and that God is still calling labourers into it.

Our mother church stands at Stand 1757 Chiwaridzo in Bindura, Mashonaland Central, and the ministry reaches beyond it through assemblies, conferences and partnerships across Zimbabwe and further afield.

The house walks through 2026 under the theme Manifestation of God's Purpose: A Year of Fulfillment, standing on Isaiah 62:4–9 — a call to move from knowing God's purpose to seeing it manifested. #yearoffulfillment
